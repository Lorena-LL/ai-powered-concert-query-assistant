After nearly a two-year break, Robbie Williams is gearing up to return to the big stages across Europe. The British artist has officially announced his "XXV Live" tour, set to kick off in October 2025, with stops in several major cities across the continent.

The tour will begin at the National Arena in Bucharest on October 3, followed by concerts in Budapest (October 5), Prague (October 8), and Berlin (October 12). It seems that Robbie is planning a complex show, with impressive visual elements and a setlist featuring hits from throughout his career.

Sources from the production team have stated that each performance will be customized to the city, with special moments dedicated to the local audience. Tickets will go on sale starting May 10.
