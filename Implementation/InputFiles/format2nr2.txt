Dua Lipa has officially confirmed she will embark on a new world tour in 2026, titled the "Radical Optimism Tour." The announcement comes after the release of her album of the same name, and it has been met with excitement by fans worldwide.

The first confirmed dates include performances in Tokyo (March 1, 2026, Tokyo Garden Theater), Bangkok (March 5), and then a series of concerts in Australia, including Sydney (March 14) and Brisbane (March 17). The tour will then head to Europe in the second half of the year, though exact locations have yet to be revealed.

In an interview, the artist mentioned that the shows will feature a retro-futuristic visual concept, along with spectacular choreography. The production team behind the show is the same group that worked on her Future Nostalgia Tour.
